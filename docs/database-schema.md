## Identity Tables

ASP.NET Core Identity creates the standard identity tables:

- `AspNetUsers`
- `AspNetRoles`
- `AspNetUserRoles`
- `AspNetUserClaims`
- `AspNetRoleClaims`
- `AspNetUserLogins`
- `AspNetUserTokens`

Custom user fields:

- `FullName`
- `IsActive`
- `CreatedAtUtc`
- `LastLoginAtUtc`

Seeded roles:

- `Admin`
- `Supplier`
- `Buyer`

## Marketplace Tables

### SupplierProfiles

Stores supplier company information and admin approval state.

Important columns:

- `UserId`
- `CompanyName`
- `CompanyDescription`
- `CommercialRegistrationNumber`
- `TaxNumber`
- `Website`
- `LogoUrl`
- `Governorate`
- `City`
- `AddressLine`
- `ContactPersonName`
- `ContactPhone`
- `ApprovalStatus`
- `RejectionReason`
- `ApprovedByUserId`
- `ApprovedAtUtc`

Important indexes:

- unique `UserId`
- `ApprovalStatus`
- `CompanyName`
- unique filtered `CommercialRegistrationNumber`

### Categories

Supports nested product categories.

Important columns:

- `ParentCategoryId`
- `Name`
- `NameAr`
- `Slug`
- `Description`
- `IsActive`
- `SortOrder`

Important indexes:

- unique `Slug`
- `ParentCategoryId`

### Products

Stores supplier product listings.

Important columns:

- `SupplierProfileId`
- `CategoryId`
- `Name`
- `Slug`
- `SKU`
- `Description`
- `Unit`
- `MinimumOrderQuantity`
- `Price`
- `Currency`
- `IsPriceVisible`
- `StockQuantity`
- `Status`
- `RejectionReason`
- `ApprovedByUserId`
- `ApprovedAtUtc`
- `PublishedAtUtc`
- `IsDeleted`
- `DeletedAtUtc`

Important indexes:

- unique `SupplierProfileId`, `Slug`
- `SupplierProfileId`, `Status`
- `CategoryId`, `Status`
- `Status`, `CreatedAtUtc`
- `Name`

### ProductImages

Stores product image metadata.

Important columns:

- `ProductId`
- `Url`
- `AltText`
- `SortOrder`
- `IsPrimary`
- `CreatedAtUtc`

Important indexes:

- unique filtered `ProductId`, `IsPrimary` where `IsPrimary = 1`

### Inquiries

Stores buyer contact requests to suppliers.

Important columns:

- `BuyerUserId`
- `SupplierProfileId`
- `ProductId`
- `Subject`
- `Message`
- `Quantity`
- `Unit`
- `Status`

Important indexes:

- `BuyerUserId`, `CreatedAtUtc`
- `SupplierProfileId`, `Status`

### InquiryMessages

Stores follow-up messages inside an inquiry thread.

Important columns:

- `InquiryId`
- `SenderUserId`
- `Message`
- `IsRead`
- `CreatedAtUtc`

Important indexes:

- `InquiryId`, `CreatedAtUtc`

### ApprovalAuditLogs

Stores admin approval actions for supplier profiles and products.

Important columns:

- `AdminUserId`
- `EntityType`
- `EntityId`
- `Action`
- `Reason`
- `CreatedAtUtc`

### RefreshTokens

Stores hashed refresh tokens for future auth flows.

Important columns:

- `UserId`
- `TokenHash`
- `ExpiresAtUtc`
- `RevokedAtUtc`
- `CreatedAtUtc`
- `CreatedByIp`

Important indexes:

- `UserId`
- unique `TokenHash`
